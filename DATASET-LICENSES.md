software and model licenses do not relicense source material.

GuitarSet attribution: Qingyang Xi, Rachel M. Bittner, Johan Pauwels, Xuzhou
Ye, and Juan Pablo Bello, “GuitarSet: A Dataset for Guitar Transcription,”
ISMIR 2018; <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3371780>.

Any new dataset must be recorded with exact version/hash, authoritative source,
license, attribution, data role, and commercial clearance before admission.
