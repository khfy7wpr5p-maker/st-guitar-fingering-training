# Contributing

Before merge, each contributor must agree to
`CONTRIBUTOR-LICENSE-AGREEMENT.md` through the repository's designated written
record. Signed-off commits alone do not replace that agreement.

Contributors must have the right to submit their work, identify third-party
materials, preserve notices, and never add confidential, personal, or
uncleared training data. Every dataset proposal must include exact provenance,
license, attribution, data role, and commercial-clearance status. Required
tests and license-policy checks must pass.
